## Bug reports

Search the
[issue tracker](https://github.com/benedictusrey/WhatsNow-for-WhatsApp/issues) before opening
a report. Use the bug-report form and include:

- WhatsNow version and package type;
- operating system, architecture, and webview version;
- the account count without phone numbers or account names;
- exact steps and the result you expected;
- the content-free WhatsNow diagnostic log when relevant.

Remove private information from screenshots. Do not upload webview profiles,
cookies, authentication tokens, chat exports, or message content.

## Feature requests

Use the feature-request form. Describe the workflow, platform, and security
boundary the change must preserve.

## Security reports

Do not open a public issue for a vulnerability, credential exposure, or privacy
problem. Follow the private reporting instructions in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
